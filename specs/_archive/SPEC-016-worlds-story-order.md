# SPEC-016 — Worlds by the story: Park, Football field, City, School + bonus Beach and Winter

Status: done (a7d76c3 + CI font fix 6084bb3)
Complexity: L
Branch: SPEC-016-story-worlds
Decided by: Kostya and Andrei (2026-11-22): the four main worlds are Park → Football field → City → School (the School ends with the grumpiest dad); Beach and Winter become **bonus worlds** that open after all levels of the main worlds are won; the bonus worlds are as hard as the School or harder. Recorded in `claude/story.md` (project doc). Everything else (themes for the two new places, seeds, migration, numbers): Claude (spec author).

## Description
SPEC-006 made four worlds of 20 levels in the order Park, School, Beach, Winter. Kostya's story now has one neighbourhood: the park, the football field, the city streets and the school. This spec adds the **Football field** and the **City** as new places, puts the worlds in the story order, and turns Beach and Winter into bonus worlds (20 levels each, locked until every main level is won). Existing Park, School, Beach and Winter maps stay exactly as they are.

## Goal
- 6 worlds × 20 levels = 120 fixed, fair maps; main order Park, Field, City, School; Beach and Winter after.
- The two new places look like themselves in every level type.
- Nobody loses progress; maps a child already played don't change.

## Worlds
- `MAIN_WORLDS = ["park", "field", "city", "school"]`, `BONUS_WORLDS = ["beach", "winter"]`, `WORLDS = [...MAIN_WORLDS, ...BONUS_WORLDS]`.
- **Seeds are keyed by the world name, not its position**: a fixed table next to `WORLDS`, `WORLD_SEED = { park: 1, school: 2, beach: 3, winter: 4, field: 5, city: 6 }`, and `levelSeed(world, n) = n <= 1 ? 1 : WORLD_SEED[world] × 100 + n`. This reproduces today's numbers exactly for Park (102…120), School (202…220), Beach (302…320) and Winter (402…420), so their maps stay the same (`levels-worlds.json` unchanged); Field (502…520) and City (602…620) get their own. Level 1 stays seed 1 in every world (the kids' drawing, SPEC-006), so Field 1 and City 1 have the drawing's layout in their own dressing. `WORLD_SEED` is not a tuning number (changing it changes maps), so it lives next to `WORLDS`, not in `CFG`; expose it as `__bb.WORLD_SEED`.
- **Difficulty index** per world (replaces the position in the formulas): park 0, field 1, city 2, school 3, beach 3, winter 4 — in `CFG` as `CFG.WORLD_DIFFICULTY = { park: 0, field: 1, city: 2, school: 3, beach: 3, winter: 4 }` (one helper, e.g. `worldDifficulty(world)`, used everywhere below). `CFG.WORLD_SPEED_START` by index: [0, 5, 10, 15, 18] (winter 18); hearts use the index (beach 9 → 12, winter 10 → 12); the cap `CFG.BIG_CHASE_MAX` stays. Coins per world (`REWARD.PER_WORLD × index` in `rewardBase`) use the index too.
- **What switches to what:**
  - difficulty index: `levelBoost`, `bigHearts` (→ `difficulty()`), `rewardBase` (`REWARD.PER_WORLD`), the fast-star time `CFG.FAST_TIME(n) + index × CFG.FAST_PER_WORLD` (its reason is the extra hearts, which follow the index), and the fast-star `target` in `scripts/balance.js`;
  - the world name (unchanged): seeds (via `WORLD_SEED`), `levelCache` / `levelKey`, `levelReport`, `progress.worlds`, `lastWorld`, `THEMES`, `I18N.*.worlds`, the balance report rows; the balance autopilot seeds `seedOf` take `WORLD_SEED[world] − 1` instead of the position, so the Park/School/Beach/Winter runs keep their seeds and the report stays comparable;
  - the position in `WORLDS` (story order): `cycleWorld`, the order of the world cards, `CFG.BALANCE.ECONOMY.UPGRADE_WORLDS` (now the first three = Park, Field, City), the report's "last world" target (`worlds.at(-1)` = Winter, as now).
- **Unlocking:** main worlds open from the start (SPEC-006 rule); inside a world levels open one by one. Bonus worlds open when all 80 main levels are won (`bonusUnlocked()` = every `progress.worlds[w].won[n]` is `true` for `w` in `MAIN_WORLDS`, n 1…20); a save that already has progress in Beach or Winter keeps them open (nobody loses what they had) — "has progress" = `unlocked > 1` or any `won`/`stars` entry in that world (`bonusOpen(world)` = `bonusUnlocked()` or that). The lock is in the menu (`selectWorld`, `cycleWorld` skips locked worlds, world card); `__bb.world` stays unrestricted (tests and `npm run balance` set any world directly). A saved `lastWorld` that is a locked bonus world (e.g. `"winter"` with no Winter progress) opens on `park` instead.

## New places
**Football field** (`field`): ground — striped mown grass with white chalk lines, centre circle and penalty boxes painted on the ground texture; edges — low advertising boards; decor — goals at both ends (movement blocked, sight passes through the net, like a pond), corner flags, benches, a ball or two (decor only); trees outside the fence. Per level type: meadow = open pitch with a few bushes; village = kiosks and locker-room huts; river = a drainage stream with small footbridges; rooms = the clubhouse with changing rooms; arena = the stands as the ring; maze = rows of advertising boards and hedges; bushes = round hedges; rocks = kit bags/cones piles; trees = lamp masts or trees.
**City** (`city`): ground — grey asphalt streets with crosswalks and dashed lines, light pavements, small squares; decor — lamp posts, hydrants, benches, bins; obstacles — parked cars (block movement and sight, like rocks); per level type: village = little town houses; river = a canal with stone bridges; rooms = a shopping centre with shops; arena = a roundabout ring; maze = yard fences and hedges; bushes = flower planters; trees = street trees.
Both get full `THEMES` entries (all wall kinds, cottage, water, bridge, maze, arena colours, square, fountain), the kids' safe house and the dad's house in theme colours (`walls.home`, `walls.bighome`), and pass the SPEC-002 fairness rules.
Pre-flight precision: the generator is theme-free and seeded only by `levelSeed`, so the simplest way is to dress existing layout items per place (`makePondFor("field")` = a goal, `makeRockFor("city")` = a parked car, etc.). If goals are added as new layout items instead, that branch runs only for `world === "field"` and must not draw from `rng` for the other worlds — the four old worlds' `generateLevel` output must not change (the fixture hashes prove it).
